Introduction

The EchoEar is an intelligent AI development kit developed by Espressif in collaboration with the Volcano Engine button’s large-scale model team. It is suitable for voice-interactive products such as toys, smart speakers, and smart central control systems that require large-scale model support. The device is equipped with the ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 module, a 1.85-inch QSPI circular touchscreen, and a dual-microphone array, and supports offline voice wake-up and sound source localization algorithms. Combined with the large-scale model capabilities of the Volcano Engine, the EchoEar enables full-duplex voice interaction, multimodal recognition, and intelligent agent control, providing a solid foundation for developers to build a complete on-device AI application experience.

Features

The EchoEar's main controller uses Espressif's ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R16VA module, supporting 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and Bluetooth 5 (LE) wireless connectivity. For storage, the device features 16MB of PSRAM, 16MB of Flash memory, and a microSD card slot supporting up to 32GB, meeting the needs of voice interaction and multimedia processing. A 1.85-inch circular touchscreen (360×360 resolution) and the ESP32-S3's native touch sensor provide an intuitive and rich interactive experience.

For audio, the Echo Ear features a built-in 3W speaker and dual microphone array, supporting local voice wake-up and sound source localization. The power supply is compatible with both 5V DC and 3.7V 700mAh lithium batteries. It also includes a USB-C port for power and program downloads, and a Pogo pin port is reserved for functional expansion.

A major technical highlight of the Echo Ear is the esp-brookesia framework, which not only handles the overall UI construction and rendering but also deeply integrates the Volcano Engine button platform and Espressif's new audio and video framework, esp-gmf , integrating multiple intelligent features optimized for the end-user. With this framework, the Echo Ear can achieve full-duplex voice interaction, multimodal recognition, and intelligent body control, creating a more immersive human-computer interaction experience.

  • Smart Intercom and Emotion Recognition

    Smart intercom and emotion recognition proactively identify user intent and emotional changes. Based on the semantic understanding capabilities of a large model, it makes a comprehensive judgment based on tone, word meaning, and context. It responds with anthropomorphic dynamic expressions and voice feedback, further enhancing the device's emotional expressiveness and personalization.

  • Long-term Memory: This feature allows for continuous recording of multiple conversations. It can remember core information such as the user's name, preferences, and frequently used phrases, and then recall it in subsequent interactions. This provides a personalized experience that's more aligned with user habits and enhances the device's interactive value as an "emotional companion."
  • Offline Voice Wake-up and Sound Source Localization: Offline voice wake-up and sound source localization combine a motor control module and a dual-microphone array to achieve precise directional tracking within a 180° range. Each time a user activates voice wake-up, the device automatically identifies the direction of the sound source and, using the rotating base, enables visual alignment, making the interaction more immersive and natural.
  • Customizable Voice Characters: Powered by the Huoshan Button platform, EchoEar supports customizable voice characters, allowing you to flexibly switch between different tones and styles to create your own unique AI voice character. Developers can customize their voice characters to create anything from a girl next door to a Bay Area man, providing a more flexible and personalized voice experience.
  • MCP protocol and Function Call capability: EchoEar also supports the MCP protocol and Function Call capability, and can connect to local smart devices to realize remote control , task distribution and status feedback functions. As the local control center in the smart home system, it provides users with stable and efficient edge control capabilities and open expansion interfaces.
  • Motion and posture perception: EchoEar has a built-in IMU sensor that can sense your movements and postures and interact with you using "body language".

  1. Install the CoreBoard and BaseBoard into the bottom cover of the enclosure and secure them with two M1.6 screws . Make sure the Type-C and pogo pins are inserted into the holes in the bottom cover.
  1. Paste the Touch copper foil to the top of the bottom cover and insert the connector into the Touch interface on the CoreBoard . Be careful not to block the gap between the front and back covers.
  1. Place the speaker in a fixed position, secure it with two M2 screws, and insert the connector into the SPK interface on the CoreBoard.
  1. Install the battery, stick it on the back of the speaker, and plug the connector into the BAT port on the CoreBoard.
  1. Attach dustproof foam to the microphone on the MicBoard.
  1. Install the MicBoard on the front cover of the housing, fix it with two M1.6 screws, and insert the FPC cable.
