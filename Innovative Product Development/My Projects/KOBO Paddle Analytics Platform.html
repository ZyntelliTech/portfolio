KOBO Paddle Analytics Platform

I designed the electronic board and developed the embedded firmware for a battery-powered system that captures pickleball paddle motion and detects where the ball contacts the paddle face.

Connecting paddle motion to mobile analytics

Built around the Nordic nRF52840 and Zephyr RTOS, the device combines an IMU with pressure sensors to capture motion and ball-contact data. I implemented sensor fusion to process accelerometer and gyroscope readings and estimate the paddle’s orientation as roll, pitch, and yaw. The firmware also processes pressure sensor readings to determine the ball’s contact position on the paddle.

The device transmits orientation and ball-contact position data to the KOBO mobile app through Bluetooth Low Energy. To support battery-powered operation, I implemented low-power firmware logic using Zephyr’s power-management capabilities.
