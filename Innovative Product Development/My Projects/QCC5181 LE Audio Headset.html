Bluetooth LE Audio Headset

A Qualcomm QCC5181 headset prototype developed by ZyntelliTech Development LLC, bringing wireless audio, a dedicated DAC, voice input, stereo output, and rechargeable power together on a custom PCB.

From wireless audio architecture to a headset prototype

The project brings together hardware architecture, schematic capture, custom PCB layout, Bluetooth audio firmware development and configuration, digital audio integration, and board-level debugging. The goal was to create a compact headset platform with left and right speaker output, a boom microphone for voice input, and integrated battery charging and power management.

Engineering challenges & solutions

Digital audio integration

Challenge

Bring the QCC5181 audio platform and a separate PCM5102 DAC into one coherent audio path, with firmware configuration aligned to the hardware interfaces.

Solution

Developed the digital audio interface and DAC integration alongside the Bluetooth audio firmware configuration, keeping the schematic connections and audio routing aligned during bring-up.

Compact mixed-signal PCB

Challenge

Accommodate wireless circuitry, digital audio, analog signal paths, user controls, and power circuitry within a compact headset board.

Solution

Designed the hardware architecture, schematics, and custom PCB layout as an integrated system, coordinating component placement and interconnect routing around the headset application.

Voice input & stereo output

Challenge

Integrate a boom microphone and separate left and right speaker connections while managing the different roles of voice capture and audio playback.

Solution

Engineered microphone and speaker signal routing together with the digital-to-analog output path, carrying the audio connections through schematic design, board integration, and prototype debugging.

Rechargeable power integration

Challenge

Support a battery-powered audio platform with charging and power management on the same compact board as the audio circuitry.

Solution

Integrated rechargeable battery charging and power management circuitry into the custom hardware, including power-system bring-up as part of prototype development.

Custom hardware, built around the audio system

  • Qualcomm QCC5181: Bluetooth audio SoC at the center of the LE Audio headset platform.
  • PCM5102 DAC: Dedicated digital-to-analog conversion for the playback audio path.
  • Boom microphone: Voice input for hands-free communication.
  • Left and right speakers: Stereo audio output.
  • Battery charging & power management: Integrated circuitry for rechargeable operation.
  • Custom PCB: Compact integration of wireless, audio, controls, and power functions.

Development results

The project produced a custom QCC5181 headset prototype platform, with hardware design, Bluetooth audio firmware development and configuration, DAC integration, microphone and speaker routing, and rechargeable power circuitry brought together through hardware bring-up and debugging.

The engineering result is a foundation for continued headset evaluation and refinement, supported by the custom PCB design and an enclosure concept. Measured audio quality, wireless range, latency, battery runtime, and final mechanical fit are not reported in this case study.
